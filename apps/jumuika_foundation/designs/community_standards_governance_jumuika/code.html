Biometric Non-Storage Guarantee

In VR/spatial computing, your involuntary pupil dilations, eye saccades, and micro-expressions can reveal deep emotional states. We believe this data belongs exclusively to your soul, not a corporate database.

Jumuika operates an ironclad edge-only processing framework. Your eye-tracking points and facial skeletal frames are converted into generic blendshape coordinates directly on your local device. The underlying raw biometric vectors are destroyed milliseconds later and never touch Swizzy Industries cloud servers.
