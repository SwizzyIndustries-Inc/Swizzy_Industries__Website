About This Gathering

Distance dissolves when sound travels without friction. The Diaspora Jam Session is a bi-continental sonic laboratory conceived to unite western Kenya's iconic polyrhythmic Benga lineage with the deep, syncopated pulses of London's underground Afro-House renaissance.

Live musicians will inhabit an interconnected virtual auditorium powered by Swizzy Spatial Mesh. Listeners will experience zero-compression acoustic presence: whether standing right beside the Nyatiti harp player in the acoustic circle or stepping outside onto the ambient balcony overlooking the virtual Nairobi skyline.
