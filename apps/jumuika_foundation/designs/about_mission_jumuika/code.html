Origin Narrative

From the Nairobi River to the World.

Silicon Valley social platforms were designed around extraction: monetizing ambient loneliness into solitary scroll feeds. They reward outrage, performative status, and individual envy.

African communal wisdom taught us an entirely distinct path. In Swahili culture, Harambee is not just pulling together in labor—it is the physical reality of communal sanctuary. The evening baraza was never a feed to be consumed; it was an open circle where elders, travelers, and children sat in mutual acoustic presence, passing warmth around an open flame.

When our diaspora sisters and brothers moved to London, Atlanta, Toronto, and Berlin, we tried staying close via fragmented group chats and glitchy video calls. But you cannot share silence on a video call. You cannot look across the room, glance together at the rain falling, or whisper across a verandah. Jumuika was forged to engineer that lost reality.

Pillar I

Family & Heritage Sanctuaries

Multi-generational virtual rooms crafted to feel like ancestral courtyards. Grandparents in Machakos or Kisumu can settle into a digital verandah, sipping tea while grandchildren in Toronto or Peckham sit across from them, telling folk stories with life-sized scale and gentle spatial warmth.

Pillar II

Silicon Savannah Infrastructure

Engineered from the ground up for resilient African network realities. Our proprietary WebRTC topology uses adaptive bitrates that maintain crystal-clear spatial audio and responsive posture streaming even on fluctuating 3G cellular connections in suburban East Africa.

hub Sub-15ms edge relay nodes in Mombasa, Nairobi, and Marseille

Pillar III

Zero-Dopamine Architecture

We eliminated the toxic machinery of modern social networks. You will never find follower counters, public like buttons, endless algorithmic recommendations, or viral leaderboards inside Jumuika. If a room has two people, it is complete. If it has twenty, it is a celebration.

Pillar IV

Radical Data Sovereignty

Strictly compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and European GDPR. Eye tracking, micro-expression cues, and spatial telemetry are rendered on-device and never stored on remote servers. What is said in your verandah remains strictly within your circle.
