Three Generations, Two Continents, One Birthday Verandah

The Mwangi family gathered for Cùcù’s 82nd birthday inside a custom Kenyan garden space. Grandmother sat with a tablet on 3G in Eldoret while cousins in London wore VR headsets—spatial audio allowed whispers across the table without interrupting the main song.

“When Wamuyu leaned in from Seattle to hand me virtual roast maize, she sounded like she was right at my elbow. My heart stopped aching for the flight tickets.”
