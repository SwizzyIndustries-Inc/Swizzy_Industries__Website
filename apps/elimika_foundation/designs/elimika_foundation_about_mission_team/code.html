Origin Story & Pedagogical Core

From Textbook Abstraction to Hands-on Fluency

The Physical Laboratory Bottleneck

Every academic year, over 1.4 million Kenyan secondary school learners and TVET apprentices encounter vital scientific concepts exclusively through two-dimensional chalkboard line art and rote memorization. The structural reality is harsh: high recurrent costs for laboratory reagents, fragile glassware, and an acute scarcity of calibrated oscilloscopes or precision vernier calipers leave over 78% of rural institutions unable to conduct weekly practicals.

When the Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) introduced hands-on inquiry as a mandatory pillar of formative assessment, the gap between urban private academies and public county secondary schools threatened to widen into an insurmountable chasm.

lightbulb

"Elimika was established to ensure no Kenyan student ever answers an examination question about the anatomy of a mammalian heart or an induction motor without having dismantled and inspected it firsthand."

Through strategic incubation under Swizzy Industries, Elimika engineers and pedagogues transformed high-fidelity WebXR protocols into zero-install modules compatible with standard Chromebooks, tablets, low-spec laptops, and projector-driven interactive classroom setups.

devices

1. Radical Accessibility on Modest Hardware

Elimika modules bypass demanding graphical runtimes. Using highly optimized WebGL shaders and compression pipelines, every interactive simulation executes smoothly at 60fps on sub-$100 standard Android tablets and refurbished laboratory desktop rigs.

fact_check

2. Strict Curriculum Rigor Aligned to KICD

Zero gamified generic science trivia. Every procedural apparatus, chemical reaction equation, and circuit breadboard directly references official Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) Junior and Senior School designs.

co_present

3. Educator-Centric Classroom Choreography

Software shouldn't replace teachers; it should arm them with superpowers. Our spatial orchestrations give instructors instant telemetry on student hypothesis testing, misconception spotting, and collaborative grouping.

public

4. Local African Heritage & Contextual Examples

Simulations ground thermodynamics in Olkaria's geothermal fields, agricultural ecology in Rift Valley volcanic soils, and logistics mechanics in the standard gauge transport networks across Mombasa and Kisumu.

Foundational Ethos

Our Four Non-Negotiables

The structural principles that govern every simulation we render, partnership we sign, and line of code we ship.

menu_book

Curriculum Before Chrome

We never produce novelty VR spectacles. Every 3D interaction must validate a measurable pedagogical competency mapped directly to national syllabus standards.

power_off

Zero Hardware Lock-In

Education should not be hostage to proprietary headsets. Elimika runs flawlessly on browser-equipped touchscreens, projectors, and modest devices without mandatory hardware bundles.

shield

Student Privacy is Sacred

Fully compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019. We never monetize learner telemetry, serve ads, or sell biometric spatial gaze data to external brokers.

person_check

Respect for Teacher Agency

We design tools that amplify the classroom teacher, not algorithmic systems that attempt to bypass them. The teacher retains complete directorial command over the lesson flow.
