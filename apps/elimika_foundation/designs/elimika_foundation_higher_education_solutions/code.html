The Academic Reality

The Resource Constraint in Tertiary Science & Engineering Practicals

Kenya's tertiary institutions face surging undergraduate enrollments alongside staggering capital expenditure requirements for physical laboratory apparatus, limiting genuine discovery-driven learning.

payments

Prohibitive High-End Equipment CapEx

Electron microscopes, supersonic wind tunnels, and HPLC chromatography units cost millions of shillings, leaving 80% of undergraduate cohorts with passive, purely observational lab sessions.

Severe Scheduling & Cohort Bottlenecks
