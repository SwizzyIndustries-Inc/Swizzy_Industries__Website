Institutional Bottlenecks

The Hands-on Learning Gap in Secondary Education

Why physical science practicals face bottlenecks across Kenya.

payments

High Consumable & Reagent Costs

Schools spend significant portions of their operational budget on chemicals, glassware, and specimen preservation that can only be utilized once, constraining repeat learner experiments.

trending_down Up to 42% of annual science budgets expended on perishables
warning

Lab Infrastructure Deficits

Over 60% of rural and county secondary schools lack dedicated piped gas, fume hoods, or safety apparatus required to safely facilitate complex STEM experiments under standard safety protocols.

balance Regional equity disparity in STEM national examinations
menu_book

Passive Memorization vs. Spatial Inquiry

Abstract textbook diagrams fail to build genuine spatial intuition for 3D biological systems, molecular orbital geometry, or multi-axis Newtonian physics concepts.

psychology Rote memorization creates disconnect in real practical tasks
