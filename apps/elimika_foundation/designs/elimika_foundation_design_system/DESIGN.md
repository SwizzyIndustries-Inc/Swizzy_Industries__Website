## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an optimistic, technologically advanced, and institutional aesthetic tailored for transformative educational environments across Kenya. Built under the vision of "Learning, reimagined in three dimensions," the interface communicates pedagogical rigor, institutional dependability, and cutting-edge immersion across Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality, and AI learning environments.

The visual tone deliberately departs from dark-mode "gamer" visual tropes, neon sci-fi hues, and juvenile, cartoonish edutainment motifs. Instead, it embodies a clean, contemporary institutional elegance that commands respect from education ministries, university chancellors, technical institute deans, and classroom teachers. The design marries spatial-computing clarity with pristine editorial composure: airy layouts, light surfaces, disciplined typography, and meaningful track-based color coding that structure multidimensional curricula with ease and purpose.

## Colors

The palette is light-mode exclusive, configured to maintain rigorous WCAG AA contrast standards across all functional and informational tiers.

### Core Brand Tokens
- **Primary / Brand:** `#3A3BD9` (Learning Indigo) - Anchor interactive element, active states, core branding.
- **Primary Hover:** `#2E2EAD` (Indigo Hover) - Deepened state for primary touchpoints.
- **Primary Tint:** `#E9E9FB` (Indigo Tint) - Subtle indicator backdrops, selected states, badge fills.
- **Accent:** `#F2B33B` (Achievement Gold) - Milestones, achievements, featured modules, callout indicators.
- **Accent Tint:** `#FCF1DC` (Gold Tint) - Ambient highlights, celebration states, soft accent surfaces.

### Neutrals & Canvas
- **Surface Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (White) - Primary content surfaces, default card tiles, clear reading fields.
- **Surface Muted:** `#F7F8FF` (Mist) - Alternating full-width section bands, secondary sidebars, input fills.
- **Border Default:** `#E4E5F5` (Cloud) - Crisp 1px boundary definition for cards, dividers, and structural containers.
- **Text Headings:** `#12132B` (Ink) - High-contrast editorial display, titles, primary labels (minimum 12.6:1 contrast ratio against white).
- **Text Body:** `#3A3C55` (Slate Body) - Body copy, descriptions, instructional passages.
- **Text Muted:** `#6B6D87` (Slate Muted) - Captions, metadata, inactive states, auxiliary timestamps.

### Curriculum Tracks
Dedicated functional identifiers to group institutional learning pathways:
- **K-12 Track:** `#3A5BD9` (Vibrant Learning Blue)
- **Higher Education Track:** `#6C4FD9` (Academic Deep Purple)
- **TVET & Skills Track:** `#1E8F6B` (Applied Technical Green)
- **Educators Track:** `#D97A3A` (Mentorship Ochre)

### Feedback & Status
- **Success:** `#1E9E6A` - Verified competencies, completed modules.
- **Warning:** `#E0A100` - Session timeouts, incomplete prerequisite alerts.
- **Error:** `#D64545` - Network interruptions, failed calibrations, invalid inputs.

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural optimism and geometric warmth of Plus Jakarta Sans for headers with the clarity and neutrality of Inter for body text, data representations, and interactive components. 

All headings and interface elements must adhere strictly to sentence case capitalization (e.g., "Virtual physics laboratory setup", not "Virtual Physics Laboratory Setup"). This supports rapid scanning, reduces cognitive fatigue, and reinforces an authoritative, academic voice. Numerals within curriculum metrics, progress trackers, and spatial measurements leverage tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve optical alignment across comparative tables.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to an 8pt base grid system framed within a centered max-width container of `1200px`. 

### Grid Configuration
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column layout, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) page margin boundaries. Content spans 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, or 12 structural columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) page margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) page margins.

### Section Rhythms
To sustain institutional polish and structural rhythm, page sections alternate strictly between `#FFFFFF` (White) and `#F7F8FF` (Mist) backdrops. Vertical section padding is established at `96px` (`6rem`, represented by `space-3xl`) on desktop, reducing gracefully to `48px` (`3rem`, `space-2xl`) on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial interfaces require razor-sharp delineation without muddy, distracting drop shadows. The design system uses crisp structural outlines paired with faint, cool-tinted ambient elevation to construct clean layering:

- **Border Strategy:** All interactive cards, content containers, modals, and tooltips are bounded by a solid `1px` border using `#E4E5F5` (Cloud).
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F8FF` with zero elevation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Static Cards & Containers):** Rested state for content modules. `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(18, 19, 43, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(18, 19, 43, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Blocks):** Subtle lift upon user focus or pointer hover. `box-shadow: 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(58, 59, 217, 0.06), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(18, 19, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Dropdown Menus & Popovers):** Elevated contextual elements. `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(18, 19, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(18, 19, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 4 (Modal Dialogs & Spatial Viewports):** Focus-exclusive overlays. `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(18, 19, 43, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is governed by a unified medium-rounded language:

- **Standard Cards, Panels, and Inputs:** Fixed `12px` border-radius (`0.75rem`), balancing structural precision with friendly tactility.
- **Buttons and Focusable Controls:** Unified `12px` border-radius to align with neighboring inputs and content units.
- **Small Badges & Track Chips:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to immediately set metadata apart from actionable rectangular cards.
- **Tooltips and Micro-indicators:** `6px` radius (`0.375rem`) for compact reading ergonomics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Exactly 48px in height, 12px border radius, horizontal padding of 24px. Fill is `#3A3BD9` (Learning Indigo) with `#FFFFFF` text (Inter SemiBold 16px). Hover transitions to `#2E2EAD` (Indigo Hover). Focused states show an external 3px outline in `#E9E9FB`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** 48px height, 12px border radius, 1px solid `#3A3BD9`, `#FFFFFF` background, `#3A3BD9` text. Hover transitions to `#E9E9FB` background fill.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** 48px height, transparent background, `#3A3C55` text. Hover shifts text to `#12132B` with `#F7F8FF` background.

### Track Badges & Category Chips
- Rendered in a distinct full-pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) with 6px vertical and 14px horizontal padding.
- Text uses Inter SemiBold 12px in pure `#FFFFFF`.
- Background matches the designated track color precisely: `#3A5BD9` (K-12), `#6C4FD9` (Higher Ed), `#1E8F6B` (TVET & Skills), or `#D97A3A` (Educators).
- Interactive filter chips that are inactive present an unfilled variant: 1px border `#E4E5F5`, text `#3A3C55`, and white background.

### Cards & Curriculum Modules
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` fill with a continuous 1px `#E4E5F5` border and 12px radius.
- Padding: 24px internal padding (`space-lg`).
- Track Association: Curriculum cards feature a 4px accent line along their top edge or an embedded track badge in the upper left corner.
- Interaction: On hover, applies Surface Level 2 elevation and changes the border to `#3A3BD9` with a 150ms smooth ease transition.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form inputs feature a 48px height, 12px radius, and a 1px border `#E4E5F5` over a `#F7F8FF` surface.
- Typography: Inter Regular 16px `#12132B`, with placeholder text in `#6B6D87`.
- Focus State: Surface switches to `#FFFFFF`, border shifts to `#3A3BD9`, complemented by a 3px ring in `#E9E9FB`.
- Checkboxes and Radio Controls: 20px dimension, 4px border radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Active state fill is `#3A3BD9` displaying a crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pip.

### VR/AR Model Viewer & AI Sandbox Frame
- Specialized card container with 12px radius, `#12132B` (Ink) interior canvas, and 1px `#E4E5F5` border.
- Controls (orbit, zoom, spatial anchor, layer toggles) float inside the viewport on frosted white pills (`#FFFFFF` at 90% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, 1px border `#E4E5F5`, and `#12132B` icons).
