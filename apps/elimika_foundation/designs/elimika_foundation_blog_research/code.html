gavel RESEARCH INTEGRITY & ETHICS PROTOCOL

Methodology & Institutional Review Board (IRB) Standards

All Elimika publications comply with Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) research guidelines, Commission for University Education (CUE) benchmarks, and the National Commission for Science, Technology and Innovation (NACOSTI).

school

KICD Pedagogical Alignment

Every spatial learning experiment measures discrete core competencies defined in the Basic Education Curriculum Framework (BECF), ensuring actionable alignment with Kenya national examinations.

security

Non-Invasive Student Privacy

Student telemetry is strictly pseudonymized at point of capture. Gaze coordinates, procedural reaction timers, and simulation steps are decoupled from personal identifiers under KDPA guidelines.

dataset

Open Reproducible Data

De-identified data sets, Socratic rubrics, and procedural logs are archived in open access repositories for university researchers, deans, and international educational economists to audit independently.
