How Pricing Works in Home & Trade

We eliminated arbitrary quotes and price inflation. You only release milestone escrow funds when the inspected stage passes your approval.

search_check
Phase 1

Standard Diagnostic / Callout

Pros charge a flat diagnostic fee (KES 1,500 – 2,500 depending on trade complexity). If you proceed with the project, this fee is deducted directly from the contract total.

Includes on-site testing, root-cause report, and official itemized quotation.
account_balance_wallet
Phase 2

Escrow Labor Milestones

Labor is split into verified completion stages (e.g. 40% Rough-In, 30% First-Fix, 30% Commissioning). Funds stay locked in the safe Nufaika Escrow until each milestone is digitally signed off.

Zero advance risk. Your funds are never released without your approval.
receipt_long
Phase 3

Zero Markup Materials

Hardware invoices and merchant delivery receipts are scanned and uploaded to your project dashboard. You pay exact vendor prices with 0% platform markup or supply materials yourself.

Partnerships with certified hardware distributors across Nairobi & Mombasa.
