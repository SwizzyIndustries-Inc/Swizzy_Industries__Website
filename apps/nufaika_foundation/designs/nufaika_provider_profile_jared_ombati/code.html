About Jared & Team

9+ Years In Field

With 9 years of dedicated field experience across Nairobi and the greater Kiambu growth corridor, Jared Ombati leads an elite team of certified electrical technicians. We specialize in diagnosing complex electrical issues, safe industrial power distribution, grid-tied lithium battery solar systems, and ensuring statutory electrical compliance for residential homes, corporate offices, and hospitality developments.

Every team member undergoes rigorous bi-annual safety recertification, holds active National Construction Authority (NCA) accreditation, and maintains strict adherence to IEE wiring regulations (BS 7671 standards).
