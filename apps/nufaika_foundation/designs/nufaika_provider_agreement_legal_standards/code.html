01

Preamble & Definitions

This Master Provider Agreement (the "Agreement") constitutes a legally binding contract entered into by and between Nufaika Kenya Limited (Registration No. CPR/2023/184920, incorporated under the Companies Act 2015 of the Laws of Kenya, operating as an enterprise pillar of Swizzy Industries Limited) and the verified specialist (hereinafter referred to as the "Master Provider", "Contractor", or "Pro").

"Client": Any natural person or corporate body hiring skilled trade or professional services via the Nufaika Marketplace engine.

"Escrow Vault": Ring-fenced trustee funds accounts maintained at tier-1 Kenyan regulated financial institutions pursuant to CBK clearance rules.

"Swizzy Industries Ltd": The parent conglomerate providing infrastructure, capital allocation, and technical dispute arbitration guarantees.

02

Provider Dignity & Independent Status

Nufaika explicitly recognizes all listed providers as sovereign independent entrepreneurs, specialized technicians, and certified business operators. Nothing in this Agreement shall be interpreted as constituting an employer-employee relationship under the Employment Act, 2007 of Kenya.

monetization_on Autonomous Pricing

Providers retain total authority over their hourly benchmarks, emergency call-out surcharges, and comprehensive project quotation breakdowns.

Providers may converse, conduct physical site surveys, and scope complex structural assignments directly with clients without platform interception.

03

Statutory Licensing & Mandatory Certifications

To safeguard Kenyan domestic residences and commercial facilities, providers offering technical, life-critical, or structurally sensitive services must maintain authentic credentials validated against national registries:

bolt

EPRA Electrical Accreditation

Class A-1, B, or C Wireman permits issued by the Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority are mandatory for solar grid connections and high-voltage building wiring.

construction

National Construction Authority (NCA)

NCA-6 through NCA-1 accreditation required for structural masonry, borehole drilling, deep-plumbing drainage grids, and load-bearing roofing installation.

badge

DCI Police Clearance Certificate

Valid Certificate of Good Conduct issued by the Directorate of Criminal Investigations within the preceding 12 months for domestic on-premise service providers.

04

M-Pesa Escrow Mechanics & 6-Digit OTP Release

Nufaika operates an inviolable settlement vault. Before physical work begins, the Client funds the agreed contract milestone in full via M-Pesa Till/Paybill or commercial card.

key The Two-Factor Work Verification Routine
  1. Physical Inspection: The Pro executes the designated scope and requests formal milestone sign-off.
  2. Secure OTP Dispatch: The system automatically delivers a unique 6-digit cryptographic PIN via SMS to the verified Client phone number.
  3. Instant Settlement: Upon the Client sharing the OTP or verifying in-app, the escrow vault initiates automated settlement directly to the Pro's M-Pesa or bank account within 180 seconds.
warning Direct cash requests prior to milestone completion violate Section 4.3 and trigger immediate suspension.

06

Hardware & Material Sourcing (0% Markup Rule)

When materials (e.g., PPR piping, circuit breakers, structural steel, marine paint) are procured on behalf of the Client:

  • Providers must upload legible, timestamped ETR tax receipts from certified wholesale merchants.
  • Material expenses are passed directly through with 0% platform commission and zero concealed markups.
  • Pros are entitled to bill a transparent "Material Procurement & Logistics Time" line item in their quotation.
07

Dispute Resolution & 24-Hour Physical Mediation

In the event of scope contestation, quality objections, or refusal to issue the completion OTP:

Tier 1 • 0–4 Hours

Escrow Freezing

Disputed funds remain securely vaulted; no automated clawbacks can occur without documented mutual consent.

Tier 2 • 4–12 Hours

Digital Adjudication

Review of geo-tagged high-definition before/after imagery, original chat histories, and contract milestones.

Tier 3 • Within 24 Hrs

Physical Engineer Audit

For projects over KES 25,000, Swizzy Industries dispatches an independent master engineer to assess physical structural integrity.

08

KES 500,000 Workmanship Bond Terms

Swizzy Industries maintains an active commercial surety pool underwriting up to KES 500,000 per verified engagement. This umbrella protects against gross contractor abandonment or accidental property damage resulting from certified tradesmen carrying active Nufaika badges.

09

Client Conduct, Workplace Safety & Anti-Harassment

Provider dignity is paramount. Nufaika strictly enforces mutual protection covenants:

Zero Tolerance Policy: Any harassment, verbal abuse, discriminatory conduct, or unsafe working site conditions imposed by Clients results in immediate contract liquidation in favor of the Pro and permanent blacklisting of the Client.

Mandatory PPE: Providers warrant they will arrive equipped with adequate protective equipment adhering to OSHA standards (helmets, insulated boots, high-vis vests where applicable).
