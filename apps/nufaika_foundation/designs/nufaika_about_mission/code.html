history_edu Our Founding Genesis

Replacing informal fragility with programmatic integrity.

In Kenya, talent has always been vibrant and plentiful. From master welders in Gikomba to licensed electricians in Westlands and certified private tutors in Karen, our communities are powered by over 15 million self-reliant tradespeople. Yet, for decades, this vast economy has operated under high friction: payment defaults, ghosting, unverified certifications, subjective pricing, and dispute deadlocks.

Nufaika was established under the vision of Swizzy Industries with an uncompromising mission: to give tradespeople the digital dignity, institutional protections, and capital respect they rightfully deserve, while liberating customers from anxiety.
