## Brand & Style

This design system drives a vibrant, two-sided service marketplace anchored in economic empowerment, skill visibility, and transactional trust. The aesthetic combines modern corporate reliability with the energetic dynamism of emerging East African digital commerce. It balances approachable human warmth with institutional stability, delivering a friction-free experience for both enterprise-grade buyers and independent trade specialists.

The visual style blends crisp modernism with tactile micro-surfaces:
- **Clean Functionalism:** Structural layouts prioritize task clarity, pricing transparency, and verification badges over decorative clutter.
- **Biophilic Grounding:** Organic greens and warm amber highlights foster a sense of growth, opportunity, and communal equity.
- **Micro-elevation & Precision Borders:** Light, breathable surfaces paired with low-contrast tinted borders establish tangible structure without visual heaviness.
- **Light Theme Exclusive:** Maintained strictly in high-clarity light mode to preserve outdoor visibility and legibility under direct sunlight across mobile devices.

## Colors

The palette leverages an intentional color hierarchy that separates transactional intent, trust validation, category navigation, and system status.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (Growth Green):** `#1E8F5C` — Anchor for primary actions, provider verification, and progression states.
  - Hover: `#166E47`
  - Active/Pressed: `#115234`
  - Tint (Surface Subdued): `#E4F5EC`
- **Secondary / Accent (Opportunity Amber):** `#F2A63B` — Used for ratings, promotional alerts, urgency tags, and visual accents.
  - Amber Tint: `#FCF1DC`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples the characterful, human geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles with the ultra-legible, functional structure of Inter for reading and UI controls.

- **Casing:** Apply sentence case strictly to all headlines, subheadings, labels, and actions. Title case is disallowed.
- **Financial & Numerical Data:** Always pair currency designations (`KES`) and rates (`/hr`) with medium or bold weights in `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter Medium` to prevent ambiguity.
- **Body Rhythm:** Default desktop body size is `18px` for introductory context and provider biographies, tapering to `16px` for standard UI copy and data tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The system follows an 8-point spatial model mapped to a fluid 12-column responsive grid.

### Container Rules
- **Standard Content Width:** Max-width of `1200px` for landing pages, informational articles, and account flows.
- **Extended Directory Width:** Max-width of `1320px` for service search directories, split map/list views, and provider catalog pages.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major landing sections enforce `96px` (`6rem`) vertical padding on desktop, reducing to `48px` (`3rem`) on mobile screens.

### Grid Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `16px` gutters, `16px` outer margins. Filter systems reflow into bottom sheets; cards stack to full width.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `24px` gutters, `24px` outer margins. Cards switch to a 2-column format.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `24px` gutters, `48px` page margins. Listings display in 3 or 4 columns; sticky filter sidebars occupy 3 columns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an organic layering model combining tinted ghost borders with ambient, green-cast diffused shadows. High-contrast dark drop shadows are strictly prohibited.

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Mist Background (`#F5FAF6`).
- **Surface Layer (Cards, Modules):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a mandatory `1px` Cloud Border (`#DFEFE3`).
- **Low Elevation (Resting Cards, Dropdowns):** `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 31, 23, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 31, 23, 0.02);`
- **Mid Elevation (Hovered Cards, Popovers):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 31, 23, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 31, 23, 0.03);`
- **High Elevation (Modals, Slide-overs, Sticky Action Bars):** `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 31, 23, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 31, 23, 0.04);`
- **Interactive Translation:** Interactive cards elevate `2px` upward on desktop hover, accompanied by a subtle transition from Cloud Border (`#DFEFE3`) to a subdued Growth Green tint (`#B6E0C8`).

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses consistent `12px` curvatures for UI containers and full-pill shapes for transient metadata tags and chips.

- **Containers & Surfaces:** All cards, dialogs, form fields, and action buttons adhere strictly to `12px` (`0.75rem`) border radius to ensure visual consistency.
- **Pill Shapes (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for categorization chips, verified status tags, star-rating capsules, and notification counter dots.
- **Avatars:** Provider profile images are circular (`50%`), framed by a `2px` white spacer border and an optional Growth Green verification ring.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Height & Dimensions:** Standard height is fixed at `48px` (`h-12`) with `24px` horizontal padding. Small variant uses `36px` height.
- **Border Radius:** `12px`.
- **Primary:** Solid Growth Green (`#1E8F5C`) background, White (`#FFFFFF`) text, font weight 600. On hover, background shifts to Green Hover (`#166E47`).
- **Secondary (Outline):** White background, `1.5px` border in Growth Green (`#1E8F5C`), text in Growth Green. On hover, background transitions to Green Tint (`#E4F5EC`).
- **Tertiary (Ghost):** Transparent background, Slate Body (`#33463B`) text. On hover, background fills with Mist (`#F5FAF6`).

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Structure:** Pure White background, `12px` radius, `1px` Cloud Border (`#DFEFE3`), Low Elevation shadow.
- **Provider Showcase Card:**
  - Header: 4:3 aspect ratio image container with category pill floating top-left and bookmark button floating top-right.
  - Body: Provider avatar (`44px` circle) overlapping media header by `16px`. Name alongside the Growth Green verified badge.
  - Meta: Review score using Opportunity Amber star (`#F2A63B`) with review count in Slate Muted.
  - Footer: Divider line (`#DFEFE3`), primary pricing anchor on the left (`From KES 2,500` or `KES 800/hr` using `price-lg`), and secondary action button on the right.

### Badges, Pills & Chips
- **Category Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `6px 12px` padding, font size `12px` (`font-medium`). Background uses the respective 10% category tint with category-colored text (e.g., Creative & Events: `#FDF1EE` background with `#E8735A` text).
- **Verified Provider Badge:** Growth Green shield or checkmark enclosed in a pill containing Growth Green Tint (`#E4F5EC`) with `#1E8F5C` label reading "Verified Pro".
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped, `8px 16px` padding, `1px` Cloud Border (`#DFEFE3`), White background. Selected state: solid Growth Green fill with white text.

### Form Inputs
- **Base Input:** Height `48px`, `12px` radius, background White, border `1px` Cloud Border (`#DFEFE3`), text Ink (`#0F1F17`).
- **Focus State:** Border color transitions to Growth Green (`#1E8F5C`) with an outer ring of `3px` Growth Green at 20% opacity.
- **Search Combo Input:** Integrated search field with location picker separated by an internal vertical Cloud Border divider and an inline `40px` Growth Green search action button.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** `20px` square, `4px` radius, `1.5px` border in Slate Muted. Checked state fills with Growth Green (`#1E8F5C`) with a crisp white checkmark icon.
- **Radios:** `20px` circle, `1.5px` border in Slate Muted. Selected state displays a `10px` inner Growth Green circle centered within a white recess.

### Trust & Pricing Displays
- **Pricing:** Always explicitly state unit parameters. Format numerical pricing as `KES X,XXX` using `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold/bold. Avoid generic dollar icons.
- **Rating Indicator:** Opportunity Amber `#F2A63B` filled star followed by rating number (e.g., `4.9`) in Ink Text, followed by review count in parentheses in Slate Muted (e.g., `(124)`).
