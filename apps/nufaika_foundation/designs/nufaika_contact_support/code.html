What Happens When You Escalate a Dispute?

Transparent, time-bound steps governed by the Nufaika Escrow Code of Conduct.

1 T + 15m
Immediate Escrow Hold

Pending milestone funds are locked securely to prevent automated release or unauthorized clawback.

2 T + 2h
Officer Evidence Review

Duty officer contacts both parties, reviews submitted photo timestamps, geo-logs, and SMS agreements.

3 T + 24h
Technical On-Site Audit

For disputed plumbing, solar, or wiring jobs exceeding KES 15,000, a certified Swizzy inspector inspects in person.

4 T + 48h
Binding Payout / Refund

Funds routed straight to buyer's M-Pesa or released to contractor based on verified quality standards.
