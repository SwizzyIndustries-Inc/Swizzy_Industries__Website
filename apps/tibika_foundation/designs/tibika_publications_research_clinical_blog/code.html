Haptic VR Procedural Simulation in Low-Resource Surgical Residencies: A Multicenter Randomized Controlled Trial.

Investigating Team: Dr. Amina Odhiambo, MD, Prof. David Mwangi, FCS(ECSA), Dr. Kevin Mwangi, MMed, Dr. Sarah Jenkins, FRCS, et al.

Objective & Methodology: To evaluate skill acquisition and structural hand-eye tactile fidelity using Tibika's decentralized 1,000Hz haptic VR surgical simulator versus conventional physical dry-lab box-trainers across three university teaching faculties in Nairobi, Eldoret, and Dar es Salaam. Over 18 months, 128 junior general surgery trainees completed modular laparoscopic appendectomy and inguinal hernia repairs under blinded objective telemetry evaluation.
