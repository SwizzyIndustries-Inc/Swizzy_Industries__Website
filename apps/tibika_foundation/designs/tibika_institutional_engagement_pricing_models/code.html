Procurement Guidance

Frequently Addressed Questions

Essential details for hospital tender boards, university finance deans, and grant administrators.

Can Tibika be procured under multilateral donor grants (e.g. World Bank, Global Fund, Wellcome)? check

Yes. Tibika holds active UNGM vendor status and complies with standard World Bank health systems strengthening capital procurement schedules. Our team routinely provides full tender specification dossiers, bill of quantities (BOQ), and benchmark compatibility tables.

How does the system function in areas with unstable hospital internet connectivity? check

The platform is 100% offline-operative. All bio-physics simulations, graphics pipelines, and scoring engines run locally on the integrated edge hardware. Telemetry logs sync seamlessly over offline USB encryption keys or whenever the system connects to a local intranet.

What happens after the initial 1-year or 3-year term concludes? check

Under CapEx purchase, you retain permanent ownership of all physical mobile carts and core software modules. Continuing maintenance and module updates can be extended via a predictable annual SLA. Under OpEx subscriptions, hardware refresh cycles are included automatically every 36 months.

Can multiple medical departments share a single Tibika deployment cart? check

Yes. The mobile cart is engineered with rapid modular tool swaps. Swapping between laparoscopic graspers, OB/GYN ultrasound probes, or orthopedic drills takes less than 60 seconds with auto-calibration, allowing General Surgery, Obstetrics, and Orthopedics to utilize the same unit across alternating shifts.
