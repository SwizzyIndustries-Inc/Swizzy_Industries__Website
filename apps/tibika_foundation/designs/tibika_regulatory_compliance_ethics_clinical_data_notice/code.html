In-Memory Pseudonymization Gate (DICOM PS3.15) STRICT_ZERO_PERSISTENCE

In accordance with Section 31 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and cross-border health directives, Tibika does not process, store, or transmit patient-identifiable markers (names, national identification numbers, hospital visit indices, or biometric features). Scans uploaded into procedural planning modules are stripped in volatile memory before 3D reconstruction begins.

Educational metrics—including Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skills (OSATS), economy of motion indices, and virtual blood-loss telemetry—are linked exclusively to pseudonymized Institutional Trainee Tokens issued directly by the participating university dean or hospital clinical education department.
