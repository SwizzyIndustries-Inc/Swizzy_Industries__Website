Clinical Problem Framing

The limitation of planar imaging in novel biomedical interventions.

Flat 2D CT and MRI slices force surgical researchers and multidisciplinary boards to mentally interpolate 3-dimensional topology. This perceptual gap obscures critical vascular branches, misrepresents kinetic shear stress, and introduces uncertainty into pre-clinical investigation.
