Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining, hosting, and building inside Swizzy Socialization spaces.

Do participants need a standalone VR headset to enter? expand_more
No. Our socialization rooms run on a progressive WebXR architecture. Participants can join directly through any modern web browser on Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, or dedicated XR headsets (Meta Quest, Pico, Apple Vision Pro). No software downloads required.
How does Swizzy safeguard against harassment and trolling? expand_more
Every user is equipped with a personal 1.5-meter spatial safe bubble, immediate avatar muting controls, and instant report keys. Furthermore, public gatherings feature designated Human Community Guardians supported by automated sentiment and hate speech filtering fine-tuned for local dialects.
How do African 3D creators monetize their virtual creations? expand_more
Verified artists can upload their 3D models, garments, and interactive virtual installations to the Swizzy Creator Exchange. Global attendees can purchase items or tip creators directly via M-PESA, Airtel Money, or international credit cards with built-in instant creator royalty splits.
Can our organization host a custom private summit or town hall? expand_more
Yes. We offer turnkey enterprise reservations with custom spatial branding, access control (SSO or whitelisted guest links), multi-track stage switching, and automated recording of the spatial sessions for archival and broadcast.
Where is user data and voice communications stored? expand_more
In compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), all authentication records, telemetry logs, and spatial assets are encrypted end-to-end and stored within local Kenyan cloud server instances. We never monetize or track spatial gaze metrics.
