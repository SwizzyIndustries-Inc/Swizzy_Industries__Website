## Brand & Style

### Personality & Vision
This design system represents a Kenyan spatial computing and immersive technology company bringing augmented and virtual realities into Health, Education, and Socialization under the banner: *"Kenya reimagined through immersive technology."* 

The brand persona rejects the flashy, neon-saturated, dark-mode tropes of speculative "metaverse" and "gamer" subcultures. Instead, it embodies **Quiet Futurism** combined with **Corporate Trust**:
- **Institutional Credibility:** Built to inspire uncompromising confidence among enterprise partners, hospital administrators, ministry and county education officials, university boards, and institutional venture funds.
- **Human-Centered & Warm:** Advanced spatial optics presented through clean, sunlit spaces rooted in modern African optimism rather than cold silicon monoliths.
- **Spatial Precision:** Interfaces echo tactile structural engineering—delivering crisp layers, deliberate optical hierarchies, and purposeful geometric clarity.

### Visual Architecture & Movement
The design movement is **Quiet Spatial Modernism**:
- **Light Theme Exclusivity:** The system operates strictly within a refined light-mode environment. Dark surfaces are reserved exclusively for contextual anchoring (such as enterprise footers and high-impact data bands).
- **Rhythmic Surface Banding:** Alternating tiers of pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and delicate mist (`#F5F8FC`) delineate conceptual sectors without relying on heavy visual dividing lines.
- **Subtle Spatial Accents:** Spatial depth is signaled through crisp 1px structural boundaries, soft directional diffusion, and a signature dual-tone Horizon wash (`#1B5FC1` to `#12A5B4`), evoking the expansive Kenyan skyline at daybreak.

## Colors

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
The palette is engineered for WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 for body copy, 3:1 for large display elements and structural controls).

- **Primary (`#1B5FC1` / Swizzy Blue):** The primary engine of the design system. Used for primary CTA actions, text links, active navigation signifiers, and focused states.
- **Secondary (`#12A5B4` / Immersion Teal):** Represents the immersion axis. Applied to secondary visual accents, reading progress indicators, and active header underlines.
- **Tertiary (`#0A1F44` / Deep Navy):** Provides high-contrast grounding. Forms the architecture for major display typography, high-impact proof bands, announcement bars, and the enterprise footer.
- **Neutral (`#334155` / Deep Slate):** Carefully calibrated for long-form readability and form control labels on light backgrounds without causing chromatic fatigue.

### Named & Pillar Colors
- **Savannah Amber (`#F2A63B`):** A rare highlight color reserved for high-value priority badges, subtle career indicators, or single-point visual draws. Never use more than once per viewport.
- **Pillar Accents:**
  - `pillar-health` (`#0E9F8E`): Dedicated to medical simulation, VR clinical workflows, and anatomical modules.
  - `pillar-education` (`#3A5BD9`): Drives virtual laboratory curricula, technical training tools, and schools.
  - `pillar-social` (`#E8735A`): Signifies collaborative spaces, cultural heritage captures, and shared spatial environments.
  - *Rule:* Pillar accents drive container tags, 4px card top indicators, and icon badges. They must **never** overwrite or replace primary interactive buttons (`#1B5FC1`).

### Backgrounds & Borders
- **White (`#FFFFFF`):** Dominant surface canvas for cards, interactive forms, panels, and primary section tiers.
- **Mist (`#F5F8FC`):** Alternating background section fill providing soft tonal rhythm.
- **Cloud (`#E6ECF4`):** The structural 1px border baseline defining input strokes, card silhouettes, and divider rules.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Display & Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Brings rounded geometric precision and modern architectural confidence to page entries, stat callouts, and section headers.
- **Body, Inputs & Labels:** `Inter`. Uncompromising micro-legibility and screen clarity across complex clinical metrics, academic guides, and high-density interface controls.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Casing:** Apply strict sentence case to headings, subheadings, and narrative elements. All-caps is reserved exclusively for the `eyebrow` token to establish structural categorization.
- **Line Length Constraints:** Paragraph blocks and editorial layouts must enforce a strict `max-width` of 70 characters (`~65ch`) to preserve reading cadence and reduce cognitive strain.
- **Hierarchy Stacking:** Always couple large section headlines (`headline-2`) with an uppercase `eyebrow` styled in either `blue-primary` or the context-relevant pillar accent.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Grid Architecture
The system employs an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-unit rhythm. Content containers adhere to three strict widths:
- **Default Container:** `1200px` max-width centered layout for standard views, hero sections, and multi-column card grids.
- **Wide Container:** `1320px` max-width for enterprise navigation headers, media showcases, and zone banners.
- **Narrow Container:** `720px` max-width centered column dedicated to long-form reading, white papers, and clinical case studies.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Columns
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, `gutter` = `1.5rem` (24px), page edge `margin` = `2rem` (32px), vertical section padding = `6rem` (96px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter` = `1.5rem` (24px), fluid page edge margin = `1.5rem` (24px), vertical section padding = `4rem` (64px).
- **Mobile (360px – 767px):** 4 columns, `gutter` = `1rem` (16px), fluid page edge margin = `1rem` (16px), vertical section padding = `3rem` (48px).

### Dimension Standards
- **Announcement Bar:** Fixed `40px` height.
- **Desktop Navigation:** Default `80px` height; smoothly contracts to `68px` on sticky scroll.
- **Mobile Navigation:** Fixed `64px` height.
- **Zone Sub-navigation:** Fixed `48px` height with contextual pillar signifiers.
- **Standard Button & Input Form Height:** Unified `48px` baseline height to preserve touch-target integrity across mobile and desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Trust Through Restraint
Elevation in this system provides structural clarity and feedback rather than dramatic theatrical volume. Skeuomorphic bevels, harsh drop-shadows, and neon glows are strictly prohibited. The illusion of spatial immersion is achieved through soft, low-contrast, cool-tinted occlusion.

### Shadow Profiles
- **Low Elevation (Resting Cards & Inputs):**  
  `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`  
  Provides tactile separation between white cards and alternating Mist surfaces without breaking planar flow.
- **Medium Elevation (Hover States & Sticky Header):**  
  `0 8px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`  
  Engaged on card interaction (accompanied by a `translateY(-4px)` lift) and underneath the sticky navigation bar once scrolled past top.
- **High Elevation (Flyout Menus & Modal Panels):**  
  `0 24px 48px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`  
  Applied to multi-zone mega menus, dialog panels, and VR media preview modals.

### Glassmorphism & Overlays
- **Navigation Surface:** When sticky over page content, navigation renders as `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)` with a `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a 1px `cloud-border` bottom rule.
- **Modal Scrim:** System dialogs sit over `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.70)`. Media galleries use an immersive `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.80)` backdrop.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Roundedness Tiers
The design system operates at **Level 2 (Rounded)** with a base corner radius of `0.5rem` (8px). Structural contours convey ergonomic precision matching enterprise spatial hardware.

- **8px (Base):** Applied to form text inputs, select dropdowns, contextual tooltips, and micro chips.
- **12px (`rounded-lg`):** The signature shape baseline. Applied to standard interactive buttons, pillar content cards, dropdown mega-panels, and masonry tiles.
- **20px (`rounded-xl`):** Reserved for macro layout panels, 3D viewport canvas containers, featured video embeds, and executive leadership cards.
- **999px (Pill / Full Round):** Reserved for category filter tags, status pills, floating badges, and the back-to-top floating control.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px` (`space-lg`), corner radius `12px`. Background `blue-primary` (`#1B5FC1`), typography `button` in pure white. Hover state transitions to `blue-hover` (`#154C9B`) over 200ms with inline trailing icon shifting `+3px` right. Focus ring: `3px` solid `rgba(18, 165, 180, 0.60)` with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary Button:** Height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px`, background pure white with a 1px solid `blue-primary` border. Text in `blue-primary`. Hover state initiates `blue-tint` (`#E4EEFB`) surface fill.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `slate-body` text color. Hover triggers `mist-bg` fill and `blue-primary` text color.

### Form Inputs & Select Controls
- **Geometry:** Height `48px`, radius `8px`, background pure white, 1px solid `cloud-border` (`#E6ECF4`).
- **Typography:** Form labels sit above the field using `small` (14px) at weight 500 in `slate-body`. Placeholder text in `slate-muted`.
- **States:**
  - **Focus:** 2px solid `blue-primary` accompanied by a `3px` offset halo in `rgba(18, 165, 180, 0.60)`.
  - **Error:** 1.5px solid `feedback-error` (`#D64545`), label remains, error message appears below input in `12px` font with a small alert icon.

### Cards & Surfaces
- **Baseline Card:** Pure white background, `12px` corner radius, 1px solid `cloud-border`, padding `24px` (`space-lg`) on desktop and `20px` on mobile. Resting elevation uses Low Elevation.
- **Interactive State:** Hover triggers a `-3px` Y-axis transformation and elevation transition to Medium Elevation over `250ms ease-out`.
- **Pillar Signature Cards:** Feature a distinct `4px` top border accent matched to the respective pillar color (`#0E9F8E` Health, `#3A5BD9` Education, `#E8735A` Socialization).

### Chips & Filter Badges
- **Dimensions:** Height `32px`, horizontal padding `12px`, corner radius `999px` (pill).
- **Appearance:** `mist-bg` background with 1px solid `cloud-border` and `13px` medium text in `slate-body`.
- **Active State:** Solid `blue-primary` background with white text, or pillar-specific tint with matching border and saturated text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry:** 20px × 20px square (radius `4px` for checkbox) or circle (for radio). 1.5px solid `cloud-border` border on white base.
- **Checked State:** Fill is `blue-primary`, hosting a crisp white checkmark or center dot. Focus ring adheres to the Immersion Teal outline standard.

### Icon Containers
- Standard icons sit inside a `48px × 48px` rounded square enclosure (radius `12px`) with a soft contextual fill (`blue-tint` or `teal-tint`). Artwork is strictly stroked (1.5px to 2px stroke width, Lucide/Phosphor style) sized to `24px × 24px`.
