Mission, Vision & Values

The institutional compass directing our engineering, partnerships, and spatial technology deployment across Kenya.

Our Institutional Mission Ratified at Nairobi HQ

“To transform Kenya’s economy by making immersive spatial computing useful, accessible, and trusted across healthcare, education, and civic community.”

Swizzy Industries operates not as a speculative metaverse laboratory, but as institutional spatial infrastructure. We deploy WebXR and precision telemetry where physical simulation reduces clinical mortality, upgrades Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) outcomes, and catalogs indigenous East African cultural heritage before historical artifacts deteriorate or disperse.

Our deployments are anchored in local hardware realities: multi-platform browser playback, sub-20ms motion-to-photon responsiveness over commercial Safaricom LTE networks, and strict offline cached mode for peri-urban and rural county institutions.
