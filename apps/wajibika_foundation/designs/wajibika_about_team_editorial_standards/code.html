Protocol Code WJB-VER-02 Triangulation Model

3-Tier Verification & Corroboration Framework

Before an issue dossier receives the official "Verified Public Petition" stamp, it must satisfy three sequential forensic milestones to prevent disinformation.

1

Physical or Cryptographic Proof

Receipts, tenders, public notices, geotagged photographs, or video files verified against spoofing and tampering.

2

Institutional Sourcing Cross-Reference

Cross-checking against the Kenya National Audit Office (KNAO), Controller of Budget, or County Integrated Development Plans (CIDP).

3

Legal Review Sign-Off

Vetting by our public interest legal panel to confirm actionable legal standing under Articles 37 & 119.

Protocol Code WJB-DEF-03 Triage Algorithm

Anti-Defamation & Malicious Filing Quarantine

We enforce a zero-tolerance boundary between protected public-interest accountability and personal slander, tribal rhetoric, or uncorroborated character attacks.

block Instant Rejection Criteria

Personal domestic allegations, ethnic slurs, leaked private communications without governance nexus, or extortion threats.

shield Protected Whistleblowing

Misuse of public assets, ghost workers on county payrolls, rigged procurement pipelines, and environmental non-compliance.

Every filing flagged for potential libel is routed to our duty counsel within 4 hours before any public ledger listing.

Takedown, Right-to-Reply & 72-Hour Appeals

Institutions, state officers, and named contractors hold an unfettered Right-to-Reply. If newly surfaced evidence invalidates a petition claim, our formal correction pipeline executes with complete public audit visibility.
