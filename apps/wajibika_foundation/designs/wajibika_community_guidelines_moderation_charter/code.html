Section 01 // The Architecture of Trust

The Four Foundational Pillars of Participation

Every submission, annotation, and verification on Wajibika must strictly conform to these non-negotiable principles of constructive civic action.

Pillar 01 fact_check

Sourcing & Evidentiary Grounding

We draw an unequivocal boundary between Sourced Public Facts and Community Grievances. Unsubstantiated speculation, hearsay, or altered media cannot be promoted into actionable institutional dockets. Every verifiable assertion must link to public record, audit citations, or chain-of-custody citizen media.

Standard: Statutory Evidence Bar Strictly Enforced
Pillar 02 balance

Dignity Over Drama

Rage-farming and pejorative personal insults destroy civic progress. Inquiries and grievances must target public office holders in their statutory capacities, administrative failures, and institutional remedies—never personal family lives, physical appearance, or protected identity characteristics.

Standard: Civil Discourse Protocol Zero Hate Tolerance
Pillar 03 flag

Absolute Non-Partisan Neutrality

Wajibika refuses political party branding, campaign rally co-optation, and electoral endorsement. Platform tools are strictly dedicated to public interest outcomes: completed roads, funded dispensaries, transparent procurement budgets, and verified legislative accountability regardless of who occupies office.

Standard: Independent Civic Utility Neutral Infrastructure
Pillar 04 security

Protected Citizen Standing

Whistleblowers and frontline witnesses deserve robust structural shields. Under Section 25 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, citizens retain an inviolable right to submit verified civic evidence under verified pseudonyms without exposing themselves or their families to extra-legal intimidation or retaliation.

Standard: Whistleblower Custody KDPA 2019 Encrypted
Section 02 // Evidentiary Gatekeeping

Evidentiary Thresholds: What is Accepted vs Quarantined

To prevent frivolous defamation while protecting vital investigative work, our moderation triage categorizes every asset according to this definitive tripartite evidentiary index.

check_circle

Accepted & Verified

Immediate Docket Escalation

Exhibits that meet formal statutory proof standards and pass metadata forensic confirmation:

  • verified Geotagged photographic or video records with EXIF timestamps intact.
  • verified Itemized procurement receipts, tender advertisements, and IFMIS transaction records.
  • verified Auditor-General published report citations and County Assembly oversight logs.
  • verified Signed CBO or baraza meeting minutes with attesting community elders.
  • verified Gazetted county bylaw references and published statutory public notices.
bolt Fast-tracked to County Executive notice within 24 hours.
pause_circle

Quarantined / Review

48-Hr Paralegal Review

Hold-state content withheld from public visibility pending secondary corroboration:

  • help_outline Uncorroborated single-source rumors or verbal hearsay lacking documented paper trails.
  • help_outline Private interpersonal disputes, family inheritance contests, and non-civic feuds.
  • help_outline Unredacted personal identifiable information (PII) of minors or vulnerable witnesses.
  • help_outline Low-resolution or stripped-metadata imagery requiring geolocation cross-referencing.
sync Submitter notified via secure inbox to provide secondary proof.
cancel

Strictly Barred

Immediate Purge & Ban

Nullified submissions triggering immediate quarantine, hash-blacklisting, and potential legal referral:

  • block Unsubstantiated criminal accusations without official Police OB or court docket numbers.
  • block Ethnic slurs, incitement to violence, and targeted hate speech under the NCIC Act.
  • block Doxxing private residential addresses, private mobile numbers, or banking credentials.
  • block Partisan electoral astroturfing, bot-driven disinformation campaigns, or deepfakes.

Section 04 // Due Process

The 4-Step Transparent Moderation & Takedown Lifecycle

Wajibika enforces rigorous due process. We reject backroom censorship, algorithmic black-boxes, and political take-down orders without published constitutional grounding.

01
memory T + 0 Seconds

Algorithmic Filter

Automated checks immediately scan text and file attachments for known hate speech lexicons, coordinated spam bot payloads, and illegal CSAM or physical threat indicators.

Output: Sanitized raw payload queued for human review.
02
assignment_turned_in T + 48 Hours

Paralegal Evidentiary Triage

Accredited civic paralegals examine jurisdiction relevance, verify county department responsibility, audit photographic metadata, and confirm the claim is grounded.

Output: Assigned to official jurisdictional track or quarantined.
03
publish Public Notice

Docket Issuance

The matter is allocated a immutable public docket code (e.g. #PET-2025-089), published live, and transmitted directly to the responsible public authority.

Output: Public tracking, Hansard linkage, open citizen endorsements.
04
history_edu 72-Hour Window

Takedown & Appeal Notice

If an institution or individual challenges an exhibit, notice is publicly registered on the docket. The submitter has 72 hours to provide counter-evidence before any modification.

Output: Transparent audit log. Zero silent backroom takedowns.
flag

The Persistent Accountability Flag

Notice a claim that violates factual thresholds or contains unredacted personal information? Every public submission contains an immediate moderation trigger icon at the upper-right corner. All community flags trigger mandatory review within 4 business hours.

Section 05 // Uncompromising Boundaries

Prohibited Content & Zero-Tolerance Thresholds

Wajibika maintains a strict cordon sanitaire against manipulative, abusive, or extra-legal conduct. Violations result in permanent hardware-level banishment and, when legally mandated, transmission to the relevant statutory investigative authorities.

no_accounts

Ethnic Mobilization & Balkanization

Attributing public malfeasance, administrative incompetence, or systemic corruption to ethnic heritage, tribal affiliation, or religious identity is grounds for instant expulsion under the NCIC Act.

badge

Doxxing & Non-Public Identification

Publishing the home addresses, personal private phone contacts, vehicle license plates of civil servants’ families, or children’s school routes constitutes severe breach of KDPA Section 72.

campaign

Electoral Astroturfing & Sybil Attacks

Coordinated campaigns deploying synthetic identities, automated sentiment swarms, or paid political brigades to distort public priorities will have all associated IP subnets permanently blacklisted.

paid

Commercial Solicitation & Extortion

Utilizing platform grievance tools to threaten commercial reputation in exchange for private payments or business contracts is classified as statutory extortion and referred to the DCI.

auto_fix_high

Synthetic & Deepfake Media

Submitting AI-generated audio recordings, altered video captures, or fabricated letters bearing counterfeit institutional letterheads is zero-tolerance, invoking immediate evidentiary revocation.

warning

Physical Retaliation Threats

Threats of corporal harm, vigilante mobilization, or arson directed at public installations or administrative workers are quarantined and escalated directly to the National Police Service.
