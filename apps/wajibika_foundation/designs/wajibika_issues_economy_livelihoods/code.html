Municipal Cesses & Multiple Taxation

Trans-county agricultural freight frequently faces duplicate charges across adjacent boundary points, violating internal market free-flow conventions and inflating retail staple food costs by up to 22%.

Sourced Citation: Kenya Institute for Public Policy Research and Analysis (KIPPRA), Inter-County Freight Barrier Review §4.2.

Informal Trader Security of Tenure

Unregulated spatial relocations and municipal askari clearance drives destabilize micro-trader inventory without the statutory 30-day consultative notification mandated by County Physical Planning statutes.

Legal Framework: Urban Areas & Cities Act (Cap 275) & Model County Informal Trading Regulations Act 2021.

Transparent County Revenue Allocation

While trading open-air centers generate over 35% of own-source county revenue, ring-fenced reinvestment into essential running water, safe drainage, and night-time security lighting remains below 6% nationally.

Audit Authority: Office of the Controller of Budget (OCOB) County Revenue Ring-Fencing Report (FY 2023/24).
