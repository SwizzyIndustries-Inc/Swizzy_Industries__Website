Statutory Grounding & Statement of Grievance

Constitutional & Devolved Governance Precedents

This citizen petition is lodged pursuant to Article 37 and Article 119 of the Constitution of Kenya (2010), which grants all persons the direct right to petition public authorities for statutory redress. It further engages Section 87 of the County Governments Act (No. 17 of 2012), mandating direct citizen participation in county resource allocations and licensing frameworks.

Under current municipal administration, small-scale grocers and informal vendors operate under conflicting sub-county bye-laws inherited from the repealed Local Government Act (Cap 265). Despite complying with daily seasonal market levies, informal stallholders face daily administrative double-jeopardy: confiscation of fresh agricultural goods without written custody records, irregular impounding fees, and lack of standardized offloading hours guaranteed by the Urban Areas and Cities Act (Cap 275).
